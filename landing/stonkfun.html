What a reward coin is

A coin that pays its holders in something else.

A StonkFun reward coin is a Token-2022 mint with a permanent 1% or 3% transfer tax. The tax is collected on every transfer, converted into the coin's quote asset, and paid pro-rata to holders. The quote can be a tokenized stock like NVDAX, a pre-IPO token, or a crypto asset like ZEC, XMR, or PEPE. Holding the coin means you are long the coin, long the quote, and earning the quote if the coin keeps trading.

Most coins do not keep trading. On 2026-09-06, 45% of about 8,000 reward coins had traded in the last day. Today it is 10.4% of nearly 88,000: about 5,900 coins launch every day, and only 3.0% of coins older than three days still trade. A holder cares about one thing: did the payout land. Nobody buys after a config audit. So the job is finding the coins that are live and paying, fast, and knowing what a trade in one of them costs.

Three jobs

Find, cost, and time a trade. Or decide what to launch.

Find what is live and paying

Every reward coin that traded today, scored 0 to 100 from a ten-minute index: recent payout, holders, market cap headroom, turnover, age, momentum, quote strength. GEM, WATCH, NOISE, DEAD, with reasons.

stonk-gems · stonk-screener · stonk-reward-risk

Cost and time one trade

Entry and exit cost at your size, tax plus price impact. Breakeven move. Your share of each payout. Expected payout over the hold, and whether it covers the round trip. Then a sell-side verdict while you hold.

stonk-quote · stonk-yield · exit-signal

Decide what to launch

Per quote asset: launches, share trading today, share paying, survival past day three, tax mix, crowding, a demand score. Then a preflight that diffs your unsigned launch transaction against StonkFun's published shape.

stonk-pairs (free) · stonk-launch-intel · stonk-launch-preflight

Questions agents ask

Straight answers, each with the call that gives it.

Is this StonkFun coin paying its holders?

stonk-reward-risk with the mint. It returns payout_status: PAYING (a payout in the last 24 hours), STALE, NEVER, or NOT_REWARD, plus hours since the last payout and the on-chain transfer-fee configuration. $0.005.

What does the transfer tax cost me on a round trip?

Reward coins carry a 1% or 3% tax on every transfer, so a buy and a sell cost 2% or 6% before slippage. stonk-quote adds the price impact at your size and returns entry cost, exit cost, the breakeven price move, and whether expected payouts over your hold cover it. $0.005.

Which StonkFun coins are worth looking at right now?

stonk-gems. Every reward coin that traded today, scored 0 to 100 from the ten-minute index and staged GEM, WATCH, NOISE, or DEAD with reasons and warnings. Filter by quote, category, age, holders, or market cap. $0.03.

Which quote asset should I launch against?

stonk-launch-intel. Per quote: launches, share trading and paying today, survival past day three, tax mix, crowding, and a demand score with plain recommendations. stonk-pairs, free, lists every quote a launch can pair against and which ones an agent can launch on. $0.02.

Can an agent use this without an API key?

Yes. Every endpoint returns HTTP 402 with the price until the request carries an x402 payment in USDC on Solana or Base. Any x402 client, the Solana Foundation pay CLI, or the SolEnrich MCP server handles it. No account, no key, no minimum.

How fresh is the data?

The index holds every reward coin that traded in the last 24 hours and refreshes every ten minutes from the StonkFun API and on-chain mint data; stonk-gems and stonk-screener answer from it in milliseconds. stonk-launch-intel answers from a full walk of every reward coin, repeated every six hours. Per-coin calls read the chain and DEX data live. Daily snapshots build the trailing yield windows, and every window says how many real days it covers.

Is any of this financial advice?

No. Every score is deterministic arithmetic over on-chain and StonkFun data, with the inputs and caveats returned beside the verdict. There is no model in the pipeline. Your agent decides; SolEnrich reports.

How to pay

Three ways in. All of them are pay per call.

From a terminal

The Solana Foundation pay CLI handles the 402 and returns the body.

$ pay curl -X POST \ https://api.solenrich.com/entrypoints/stonk-gems/invoke \ -H 'content-type: application/json' \ -d '{"limit": 5, "format": "llm"}'

From code

Wrap fetch with @x402/fetch and a Solana or Base signer, then call the same URLs. The 402 body lists every endpoint and price.

llms-full.txt has every input with types and defaults. The docs page has the schemas and scoring methodology.

From Claude, Cursor, or any MCP client

Point the client at https://api.solenrich.com/mcp. Tools stonk_gems, stonk_quote, stonk_reward_risk, stonk_launch_intel, and the rest appear with their inputs. Unpaid calls return payment instructions instead of data.
